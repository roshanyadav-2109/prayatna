Our work

Student Innovation & Entrepreneurship

We expose school students to frontier sectors, support the people who want to start something, and connect the institutions that train them with the industries that will hire them.

  • Student–industry exposure, beginning with Andhra Pradesh’s industrial clusters
  • Startup ecosystem development and entrepreneurship programmes
  • Innovation policy
  • Industry–academia collaboration and faculty development programmes

Government Advisory

We give decision-makers independent analysis and practical tools to carry decisions through, including policy briefs, development roadmaps and frameworks for checking results.

  • Policy research, policy briefs and regulatory assessments
  • Development roadmaps and performance frameworks
  • Programme evaluation
  • Training and capacity building for government officials

Areas of work

  • Policy research & strategic advisory

    Independent analysis, policy briefs, development strategies, regulatory assessments, and evidence-based recommendations for institutions and decision-makers.

  • Innovation & entrepreneurship

    Startup ecosystem development, entrepreneurship programmes, innovation policy, industry–academia collaboration, and initiatives that expand access to opportunities.

  • Strategic partnerships & institutional coordination

    Stakeholder engagement, public–private partnerships, institutional liaison, knowledge exchange, and collaboration across government, business, academia and civil society.

  • Inclusive & sustainable development

    Research and initiatives addressing livelihoods, employment, social well-being, environmental sustainability and balanced regional development.

  • Governance & institutional capacity

    Development roadmaps, performance frameworks, institutional capacity building, programme evaluation, and practical tools to strengthen implementation.
