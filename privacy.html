Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026 · Last updated: 26 October 2026

1. Introduction and scope

1.1 This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) is published by Prayatna Policy Innovation Centre (“PPIC”, “we”, “us” or “our”), having its registered office at [registered office address], in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and the rules made thereunder, to the extent in force, the Information Technology Act, 2000 (“IT Act”), and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (“SPDI Rules”).

1.2 This Policy governs the processing of personal data in connection with the website located at www.ppicindia.org and any associated domain or sub-domain operated by PPIC (the “Website”). It does not apply to any third-party website, platform or service to which the Website may link.

1.3 By accessing or using the Website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. Where processing is based on consent, such consent shall be sought in the manner required under the DPDP Act.

2. Definitions

2.1 In this Policy, “personal data”, “processing”, “Data Fiduciary”, “Data Principal”, “Data Processor” and “Board” shall have the meanings assigned to them under the DPDP Act. “Sensitive personal data or information” shall have the meaning assigned to it under the SPDI Rules.

2.2 For the purposes of the Website, PPIC is the Data Fiduciary in respect of personal data processed by it or on its behalf.

3. Personal data processed

3.1 Data you provide. The Website does not contain registration, login or data-collection forms. Where you voluntarily contact PPIC by email or other means published on the Website, we process the personal data you choose to provide, which may include your name, contact details, organisational affiliation and the contents of your communication.

3.2 Technical data. When you access the Website, our hosting and content delivery service providers automatically process limited technical data, which may include your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, device and operating system information, the pages requested, referring address, and the date and time of access. Such data is processed for the purposes of delivering the Website, maintaining its security and preventing misuse.

3.3 Cookies and tracking. The Website does not use cookies, web beacons, analytics services, advertising technologies or any other tracking technologies, and does not build profiles of visitors. All fonts and assets are served from the Website itself. Should this change, this Policy shall be updated before any such technology is deployed and, where required, your consent shall be obtained.

3.4 PPIC does not knowingly collect sensitive personal data or information through the Website.

4. Purposes and grounds of processing

4.1 Personal data is processed only for lawful purposes and only to the extent necessary, namely to: (a) respond to enquiries and communications initiated by you; (b) operate, maintain and secure the Website; (c) comply with obligations under applicable law, and with orders or directions of any court, tribunal or competent authority; and (d) establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

4.2 Processing is undertaken on the basis of your consent, or for a legitimate use recognised under Section 7 of the DPDP Act, including where you have voluntarily provided your personal data for a specified purpose and have not indicated that you do not consent to its use.

4.3 Personal data shall not be sold, rented, or used for marketing or advertising purposes.

5. Disclosure and Data Processors

5.1 Personal data may be disclosed only: (a) to Data Processors engaged by PPIC, including hosting and content delivery providers, under a valid contract and solely for the purposes set out in this Policy; (b) where required by law, or pursuant to a lawful request of a government agency, court or other competent authority; or (c) where necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of PPIC, its users or the public, as permitted by law.

5.2 PPIC remains responsible for compliance with the DPDP Act in respect of processing undertaken by any Data Processor on its behalf.

6. Transfer outside India

6.1 Our service providers may process technical data on servers located outside India. Any such transfer shall be made only to a country or territory not restricted by the Central Government under Section 16 of the DPDP Act, and subject to reasonable safeguards.

7. Retention

7.1 Personal data shall be retained only for so long as is necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required under applicable law, after which it shall be erased or anonymised, in accordance with Section 8(7) of the DPDP Act. Technical data processed by service providers is retained in accordance with their respective retention schedules for security and operational purposes.

8. Reasonable security practices

8.1 PPIC implements reasonable security practices and procedures, including technical and organisational safeguards, appropriate to the nature of the personal data processed, in accordance with Section 8(5) of the DPDP Act and Rule 8 of the SPDI Rules. The Website is served exclusively over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8.2 In the event of a personal data breach, PPIC shall intimate the Board and each affected Data Principal in the form and manner prescribed under the DPDP Act and the rules made thereunder.

9. Rights of Data Principals

9.1 Subject to the DPDP Act and the rules made thereunder, you have the right to: (a) obtain a summary of the personal data processed and the processing activities undertaken (Section 11); (b) seek correction, completion, updating and erasure of your personal data (Section 12); (c) withdraw consent at any time, with the same ease with which it was given, without affecting the lawfulness of processing prior to such withdrawal (Section 6(4)); (d) have readily available means of grievance redressal (Section 13); and (e) nominate any other individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity (Section 14).

9.2 Requests may be made to the Grievance Officer named in Clause 11. PPIC may verify the identity of the requester before acting on a request.

9.3 Data Principals shall comply with the duties set out in Section 15 of the DPDP Act, including the duty not to furnish false particulars or register false or frivolous grievances.

10. Children

10.1 The Website is not directed at children. PPIC does not knowingly process the personal data of a child, as defined under the DPDP Act, without verifiable consent of the parent or lawful guardian, and does not undertake tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising directed at children.
